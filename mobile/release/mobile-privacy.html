Privacy · iOS & Android

Your information,
through every part of the trip.

This policy explains how Destination Paradise handles information in our mobile app for phones and tablets. It covers saved places, trip preferences, AI planning, quote requests, content reports, maps and weather.

Last updated: 5 September 2026 · Contact: info@yournexttriptoparadise.com

  • You can explore without an account. Saved places and trip preferences are stored on your device.
  • AI planning is optional. We ask permission before sending preferences and chat messages to Destination Paradise and Anthropic.
  • A quote request or AI-content report is shared only after you review it and submit it.
  • The app does not request your GPS location, address book, camera, microphone or photo library.

Who handles your information

Destination Paradise, Zanzibar, Tanzania, operates the app and uses information to provide travel discovery, planning and enquiry services. Our team can be reached at info@yournexttriptoparadise.com.

This mobile policy works alongside our website privacy policy. Website cookies, browser planner storage and online checkout are separate from the native app behavior described here.

This policy, weather and AI reply reports use our dedicated Netlify site, destination-paradise-mobile.netlify.app. Planner chat and quote requests use our main website's Netlify backend at yournexttriptoparadise.com, which also hosts travel information and support links.

What stays on your device

The app stores saved destination IDs and your chosen travel month, nights, party counts, budget category, pace, interests, trip style and planning destination. This lets you return to your trip choices. Explore searches and filters are handled in the app.

Planner conversations and contact form details are held in the current app session, rather than saved to the app's local preference store. Resetting the conversation or ending that session removes the app's in-memory copy. Closing a screen or putting the app in the background may keep the session active.

You can reset saved places and preferences in App information → Clear saved trip data. This does not remove an enquiry, report or conversation already sent to a service. Photos and map tiles may remain in normal display caches. Operating-system backups are controlled by your device settings.

Optional AI trip planning

Before you begin, the app asks you to agree to share trip preferences and planner messages with Destination Paradise and Anthropic. Our backend, hosted by Netlify, passes recent chat context and your current trip preferences to Anthropic to generate a response.

The planner may ask contact questions. If you type your name, email, phone number or other personal details in chat, those details can be sent to Anthropic as part of the conversation. Contact details entered in the separate quote form are not automatically added to AI chat requests. Keep passport, payment-card and sensitive personal information out of the chat.

AI responses are suggestions and can be inaccurate. Chat does not itself make a booking, take payment or send a quote request to our team. You can choose not to use AI planning and continue using the other discovery features.

When you request a quote

You can review your draft and conversation before choosing Send to the team. The quote form asks for your name and email address, with phone or WhatsApp optional, and asks separately for permission to share those details and the reviewed conversation.

When you tap Send quote request, our Netlify service sends your contact details, travel preferences and conversation through Resend to the Destination Paradise team. It also attempts to send a copy to your email address. We use this information to respond, prepare and follow up on the quote you requested.

An enquiry does not automatically confirm a trip. Any later booking, supplier coordination or payment is handled under the applicable website and booking terms.

Reporting an AI reply

Use Report reply beneath an AI response to open Report AI reply. You can flag harmful or offensive content, inaccurate or misleading content, a privacy concern, or another concern.

The report form shows the selected AI reply, your reason and any optional notes. After you agree to share them and tap Submit report, our Netlify service sends those items through Resend to our team for safety and quality review. No other chat messages or separate contact fields are automatically added. The selected reply or your notes may nevertheless contain personal information, so review what you are submitting.

Reports help us review problems and improve planner safety and guidance. Reporting is optional and does not create a booking request. The confirmation acknowledges submission for review; it does not promise a personal response.

Maps, photographs and weather

The interactive map loads tiles from OpenStreetMap services. Destination photographs load from the Destination Paradise website. Systems serving these resources receive normal network information such as an IP address, app or browser information, and the resource requested. Map requests identify the area being viewed, not your device's GPS position.

Weather information is supplied by Apple Weather through WeatherKit. The app requests readings from Destination Paradise's weather service, hosted on Netlify. Our server requests weather for a fixed Zanzibar location from Apple; it does not forward your device's location or contact details. Apple receives the server's weather request, rather than a direct weather request from your phone or tablet.

Our hosting service receives the normal network information needed to answer your request. The fixed Zanzibar coordinates do not represent your current physical location, and the app does not request location permission for weather.

The Apple Weather logo is included with the app. If you open its legal-attribution link, Apple's website handles that visit separately. The app credits its map and weather sources and uses normal display caching. It does not offer downloads of whole maps for offline use.

Security and technical information

Our backend uses request information, including IP addresses, to limit abusive traffic and maintain the service. Hosting and service providers may keep operational logs. Backend error monitoring may use Sentry for technical context such as the service that failed and an upstream error response. We aim to avoid putting conversations or contact details into technical logs.

The app's service requests use HTTPS. We use validation, request-size limits and access controls to protect information. Email and online services cannot guarantee absolute security, so please avoid submitting information that is not needed for your travel request or report.

This native release does not include an advertising or analytics integration. We do not sell your personal information.

Sharing and how long information is kept

We use the service providers described above to operate the app, generate AI suggestions, deliver messages and diagnose faults. Your information may be processed outside your country by these providers. Their own service terms and legal obligations may affect processing and retention.

We retain enquiries and quote correspondence for as long as needed to answer and follow up on your request, arrange the trip where you proceed, resolve disputes and meet applicable accounting, legal or safety duties. We retain content reports for as long as needed to investigate the concern, improve safety, address repeated abuse and meet applicable legal requirements. Technical records are kept as needed for operation, security and troubleshooting.

These criteria apply to our handling of records; clearing your device does not erase provider logs or email already delivered. We may share information if required by law or necessary to address a safety or security issue. Information needed for a later confirmed trip may be shared with the relevant travel suppliers as described in our website policy.

Your choices and privacy requests

You can browse without creating an account, decline AI planning, leave optional contact or report fields empty, and clear locally saved trip data. You can stop sending further AI messages at any time. A request to delete information already transmitted is handled separately.

Contact info@yournexttriptoparadise.com to ask for access, correction or deletion of information you have shared, or to raise a privacy concern. Tell us which enquiry or report your request concerns and provide only the information needed to locate it. We may need to verify that the request relates to your information. Where records must be retained for legal, accounting, safety or dispute purposes, we will explain the relevant limitation.

The app has no user-account system. Resetting locally saved data and requesting deletion of off-device records are available without an account.

External pages and policy updates

Experience, booking, venue, event and other external links open services outside the native app. Their privacy notices and any cookie choices apply to your use of those pages.

We may update this policy when the app or its services change. The date at the top identifies this version. Material changes affecting optional sharing will be reflected in the relevant app disclosures and choices.
